# Bodian Music

> Bodian Music (bodian music) is a music video app developed by Beijing Kuwo Technology Co., Ltd., launched on December 14, 2020, supporting Android and iOS platforms, and is part of Tencent Music Entertainment Group (TME). The app features AI-powered personalized music recommendations as its core function, offering a library of 30 million tracks covering pop, classical, anime, and other genres, with free online streaming and downloading available[^1]

## Compatibility Notes

Please install the [bodianhelper plugin](https://github.com/Jerry-Z07/bodianhelper) and follow the instructions in the project page.
